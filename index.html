Ready?

Move the paddle with mouse, touch, or arrow keys. Press Start to serve the ball.

How to play

  • Move the paddle: mouse, touch drag, or Left / Right arrow keys (A / D also work).
  • Break every brick. Tough bricks (darker edge) need 2 hits.
  • Lose the ball below the paddle and you lose a life. You start with 3 lives.
  • Level 3 has holes in the wall — use them to aim, not as an excuse.

Breakout BD is a 100% offline toy: no account, no network requests, no data leaves your device. Best score, language and sound settings are stored only in your browser's localStorage. Ball speed rises slightly each level; this is a casual game, not a physics simulation.
